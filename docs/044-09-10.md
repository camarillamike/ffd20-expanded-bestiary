# 0.1.44: groups 09 and 10

Each review uses the cached publisher page and the actor after real reference-pack hydration. The focused test builds in memory; it does not verify artwork, a native running Foundry world, or packaging. Target effects are GM-applied unless stated otherwise.

## Group 09

- [Elite Skeleton](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/undead/skeleton-family/elite-skeleton/): equipped greatsword and chain shirt; alternate paired claws. DR 10 requires both good and bludgeoning. Corrected initiative feat typo. Black Cloud 5d4 always hits the area; Fort 15 only avoids blindness. Hell Slash primes the next successful sword hit; the extra shadow die is not multiplied and Strength loss is not HP damage. Marrow Drain has its own MP roll and three uses.
- [Gargoyle](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/monstrous-humanoids/gargoyle-family/gargoyle/): claws, bite and gore at +9; Bloody Claw is a separate melee touch, 3/day, 3d6 shadow, actual damage healed, plus one random ability damaged. Freeze permits taking 20. Rocky Stealth +16 and Strong against lightning are conditional, distinct from Earth resistance 15 and wind weakness.
- [Gather](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/aberrations/gather-family/gather/): four tentacle attacks, 10-foot reach; reflect checks targeted spells against SR 17. Flash and Para-Stare have separate Will 15 saves, ranges and durations. Self-Destruct kills the user below half HP; split one 7d6 result between physical and fire before applying resistance. No invented Strength buff.
- [Grat](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/plants/grat-family/grat/): four seeds at one target, 30-foot range, no provoking; separate 60-foot ranged-touch Seed Cannon 2d6, 5/day. Tundra Stealth and Point-Blank Shot are optional bonuses, not unconditional adjustments.
- [Great Tortoise](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/magical-beasts/turtle-family/great-tortoise/): bite +13, 10-foot reach. Water breath is a 30-foot cone for 4d6, Reflex 17 half, one charge recharging in 1d4+1 rounds. Shell is a move action with a separate +8 AC/+4 Reflex stationary state; no attacks while withdrawn.
- [Greater Mantis](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/vermin/mantis-family/greater-mantis/): printed BAB +3, claws +6 x3 crit, CMB +8 or +12 grapple. Lunge is a full-round single attack at +10 with double reach, not a doubled full attack. Mandibles +4 after grab deals 1d8+2. Conditional forest Stealth is +15; surprise permits a full round.
- [Green Wisp](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/fey/wisp-family/green-wisp/): +12 touch Shock, Wis CL 8. Chart plus ability gives 22 MP, not printed 25. Elemental Focus adds +1 DC to the three applicable hydrated wind spells, not to Fiddle. Fiddle is Will 16, staggered, 24-hour immunity on success; no printed duration. Ice spells bypass immunity; either an attack or a hit suppresses invisibility for 1d4 rounds.
- [Judge](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/humanoids/judge-family/judge/): native Knight as Judge Magister 5, d12/full BAB. Masterwork sword +11, plate +9, shield +2, Dex cap +2 with Armor Training: AC 23/touch 12. The basic projection omits the trained cap; the test separately evaluates both worn items. Fort/Ref/Will +9/+4/+5. Single pools for three Determination modes (1/day), Judgement (2/day), Might (printed 7/day). Badge talents included, not stacked. The draft supplies Antagonize's Diplomacy and Intimidate actions. The native compiler embeds the published Antagonize feat and preserves those actions and their roll notes; it is not a generated fallback. One unnamed language remains unspecified. Historical mechanics use the [published archetype PDF](https://www.finalfantasyd20.com/pdf/Judge_Magister.pdf).
- [Kefka Palazzo (Narshe)](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/bosses/kefka-palazzo-narshe/): Black Mage 5, printed BAB +5 despite class progression, dagger +1 and lightning staff +5 touch. Class pool 17 including Extra MP. All 24 spells hydrate; printed Enduring Elements uses Endure Elements. Two potions, one Ether. Shield 4 (9/day), Seal 1/day, Enhancement 3/day and a shared Limit Break pool. Black Magery uses the actual Increased Damage +2 selection; central per-spell normalization is a separate verification surface. Five languages are unnamed. Reference: [Black Mage](https://finalfantasyd20.com/classes/core-classes/black-mage/).
- [Killer Shark](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/animals/shark-family/killer-shark/): Huge, bite +12, 18-20, reach 10. On-hit grapple +19, bleed 1d6, and Fort 17 vs Deprotect are separate. Hydrotwister's Fort save prevents statuses only; still take 6d6 water. Swallowed prey faces 2d6+10, AC 14, 11 HP to escape. Reteethe has three mutually exclusive four-round Strength tiers.

## Group 10

- [Lab Rat](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/animals/rat-family/lab-rat/): Small magical beast, climb/swim use Dex; disease and poison status immunity, not immunity to a damage element. Bite and paired claws +10. The 1d3-round Critical Attack multiplies the bite and still transmits disease. Fort 16 filth fever: onset 1d3 days, daily saves, independent Dex/Con damage, two consecutive saves to recover. Bacteria is separate: 1/day, 30 feet, Fort 16 vs Sap 1d8 rounds.
- [Large Air Elemental](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/outsiders/elemental-family/air-elemental/large-air-elemental/): two 10-foot slams +14, 1d8+4 physical and 1d6 wind each. Air Mastery affects airborne attackers only; Earth 50% is not flat DR. Zephyr Arrow +14 ranged touch, 60 ft., 3d6 wind, Fort 16 vs push, recharge 1d2. Whirlwind (1d3) covers two distinct Reflex 18 saves, carrying, repeated damage and escape; see [Whirlwind rules](https://www.aonprd.com/UMR.aspx?ItemName=Whirlwind). Int SC CL 8, 14 MP.
- [Large Arcane Elemental](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/outsiders/elemental-family/arcane-elemental/large-arcane-elemental/): Con-derived +4 deflection applies once: AC 23, touch 20, CMD 33. Physical slams plus 1d8 non-elemental. Sonic Tail 20-foot radius, 5d4, Reflex 16 half/no daze; independent Fort 16 vs shattering for petrified targets only. Deaf targets exempt. Ruin hydrates. No indexed Leaf spells: localized [Leaf](https://finalfantasyd20.com/magic/spells/l/leaf/) and [Leaf II](https://finalfantasyd20.com/magic/spells/l/leaf-ii/) have four seasonal ranged touches and a seasonless 1d4/3d4 mode without bonuses. Wis SC CL 8, 14 MP.
- [Large Earth Elemental](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/outsiders/elemental-family/earth-elemental/large-earth-elemental/): paired +14 slams, 2d6+7 bludgeoning plus 1d8 earth. Choose grounded +1 or airborne/waterborne foe -4; the same situational modifiers apply to bull rush and overrun. Earth Glide leaves no tunnel; move earth displaces and permits Fort 15 against stun. Rock Throw +6 ranged touch at 30 feet, 3d6+6 earth, Fort 15 vs Slow 1d4, recharge 1d4. Cha SC, 12 MP.
- [Large Fire Elemental](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/outsiders/elemental-family/fire-elemental/large-fire-elemental/): two +13 slams, each 1d8+2 physical plus 1d8 initial fire; do not roll the initial fire twice. Reflex 18 against continuing burn, including natural/unarmed contact. Fireball is its own 720-foot, 20-foot-radius 5d8 attack, Reflex 16 half, 1d4 recharge; not the compendium spell. Wis SC, 14 MP.
- [Large Flan](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/oozes/flan/large-flan/): the page's Ice-Flan.gif supports Ice as the native Large Flan. All eight actors have distinct asset names, one elemental spell per level, their own immunity/vulnerability, physical half resistance, and forward-element half resistance where defined. Divine/Dark have only physical half resistance. Cha FC CL 7, 18 MP. Slam +10, 2d6+9 physical plus 1d6 of the selected type. Critical Attack multiplies the physical roll only, every 1d4 rounds. No trip. In build order the names (also asset names) are Large Flan, Fire Large Flan, Wind Large Flan, Earth Large Flan, Lightning Large Flan, Water Large Flan, Divine Large Flan, Dark Large Flan.
- [Large Holy Elemental](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/outsiders/elemental-family/holy-elemental/large-holy-elemental/): printed slam +9 retained. Three simultaneous ranged touches each deal 2d6 holy; Dizzy Vision is a separate Fort 16 against dazzled and also follows holy spells. Bright-light invisibility survives attacks. Daylight Door 3/day; refund inadequate destination. Two named languages. Printed 18 MP, chart plus Wis gives 15. Printed base spell DCs 16/17 become 17/18 for the monster's selected Holy focus.
- [Large Ice Elemental](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/outsiders/elemental-family/ice-elemental/large-ice-elemental/): 20-foot burrow limited to nonmagical ice/snow, swim 60. Ice Block at 65 feet, 3d6, Fort 16 half/no Immobilize, recharge 1d4. Escape uses a full-round Str 18/Escape Artist 23 or breaking 12 HP of ice. Independent Fort 18 vs staggered whenever ice damage lands. Cannot be tripped; two additional languages unspecified. Wis SC, 14 MP.
- [Large Lightning Elemental](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/outsiders/elemental-family/lightning-elemental/large-lightning-elemental/): +13 ranged touch Blaster 3d6 at 30 feet, Int-based Fort 17 vs Immobilize, recharge 1d4. Metal Mastery applies +3 only against metal; Spark Leap's +10 is restricted to four charge maneuvers. Printed 17 MP; chart plus Int gives 14. Three additional languages unspecified.
- [Large Shadow Elemental](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/outsiders/elemental-family/shadow-elemental/large-shadow-elemental/): slam +9 plus 1d8 shadow; Fort 16 vs Dimmed Vision upon shadow damage, including spells. Speak No Evil +11 ranged touch 30 feet, 5d6, Will 19 vs Silence only; 1d4 recharge. Shadow Blend is 20% concealment, not invisibility. Cha SC gives 15 MP vs printed 18. Unlike Holy, the printed Dark spell DCs 17/18 already include focus; base 16/17 plus the owned action bonus, once.
