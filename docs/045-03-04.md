# v0.1.45 groups 03-04

Fresh cached canonical HTML, parsed actors, live central dispatch, real compressed reference documents and source comparison: `node tools/test-045-03-04.mjs` passed 20 actor cases. This is a builder/hydration test, not an interactive Foundry session. Qualified effects require selection on the relevant actor or target. No extra actor/art variants. Source conflicts and verifier limitations are called out below.

### 1. [White Nakk](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/magical-beasts/nakk-family/white-nakk/)

Bite +11, 2d6+7 plus noncritical 1d6 ice and free trip. Blood Howl is a recipient toggle (+2 dodge/attack), Greater Bestial Fang three/day, and Howling Moon has a separate extra attack and nonlethal upkeep.

### 2. [Winter Wolf](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/animals/wolf-family/winter-wolf/)

Bite +11, 1d8+7 plus 1d6 ice and free trip. Breath: 15-foot cone, 6d6 ice, Reflex DC 17 half, recharge 1d4+1. Scent tracking is situational.

### 3. [Bashura](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/monstrous-humanoids/wendigo-family/bashura/)

Two +15 slams; +8 situational snow Stealth replaces base, not +12 stacked. Raise Dukes 1/day; counter a qualifying attacker once per round using one slam. DR 10/- and bull-rush immunity apply only in stance; adjudicate on incoming attacks.

### 4. [Black Lizard](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/magical-beasts/lizard-family/black-lizard/)

HP 78; bite +13 and tail +7. Separate 20-foot earth breath (6d6, Reflex 18, 1d4) and 30-foot Sandstorm (6d6 wind plus 6d6 earth, Fort 17, 1/day).

### 5. [Bloody Head](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/undead/evil-head-family/bloody-head/)

Chill 2/day lasts eight rounds. Its owned buff, not its activation, rolls the 1d6 ice and Fort 16 stagger rider if damage was taken. Test verifies those fields explicitly; generic same-item check cannot see them. Dust 2/day, Fort 16; Pumpkin Head +9 touch, capped missing-HP formula, 1d6+1 recharge. CL 8, 14 MP.

### 6. [Bone Dragon](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/undead/skeleton-family/bone-dragon/)

85 HP, bite +13; Gaze Will 17 twice/day and Horror Cloud Fort 17 three/day. The canonical Feats line lists both Improved Toughness and Toughness, and omits the comma in "Night Stalker Sow Terror". Night Stalker embeds optional PF1e Content 11.4.0, pf-feats/WlySDcs17cy9t6iT, with situational darkness/flat-footed modifiers. [Sow Terror](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/feats/monster-feats/sow-terror-monster/) is a separate standard action after an opposed Stealth win by 5: Will 17 or shaken 1d4 rounds. The actor has no combined feat. Improved Toughness (Complete Warrior p. 101) has no exact document in either installed FFd20 index or the optional PF feat pack; it is a source-keyed local feat with an actual +HD max-HP change. Independent hydration checks the decomposition: racial HD item HP 45 + 10 x 2 Cha + 10 Toughness + 10 Improved Toughness = 85. Increasing HD scales the feat; it is not counted twice.

### 7. [Brass Quadav](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/humanoids/quadav-family/brass-quadav/)

Dark Knight 7, CL 3, 7 MP; saves 14/6/12 with Dark Blessing applied once. Sword +12/+7, 1d10+4. Harm Touch 4d6, Fort 14 defile, seven charges/day shared with its alternative modes. Ten real spells. The statblock's eleventh spell, Demonhide, was [removed by the publisher](https://finalfantasyd20.com/more-goodies-another-update/); the similarly named class feature is not a spell. Retained a noncastable notice; historical spell text not verified. The unidentified level-two scroll does not invent a spell.

### 8. [Clockwork Soldier](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/constructs/clockwork-family/clockwork-soldier/)

AC 20/14/16 including +2 dodge; halberd +18/+13 (1d10+14, x3) or two +17 claws. Latch +2 disarm, no AoO; sleeping/standby and wake initiative +4 are separate states. Endurance 16 active days.

### 9. [Copper Quadav](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/humanoids/quadav-family/copper-quadav/)

Thief 7; AC 20, touch 13, flat-footed 18. The verifier declines enhanced armor, so a separate check uses the native equipment formula armor.value (4) + armor.enh (1) with Dex 2, ring 1, shield 1, natural 1. +1 short sword +9, pistol +6/+6, 4d6 Sneak Attack. Enhanced armor has zero ACP. Replaced the two malformed reference changes for Trapfinding and Improved Theft: checked Disable Device 13, Perception 12, Sleight of Hand 13, with +3 trap-location Perception separate. Point Blank Shot is the installed Point-Blank Shot feat with a verified source ID. Shell DR, injury and bleeding are conditional.
