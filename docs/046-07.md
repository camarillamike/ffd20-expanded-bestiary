# v0.1.46 group 7

Reference cross-checks: the [Kensai rules](https://finalfantasyd20.com/classes/hybrid-classes/samurai/archetypes/kensai/) retain the named legacy Kenki finishers; [Bushido Arts](https://finalfantasyd20.com/classes/hybrid-classes/samurai/bushido-arts/) distinguishes paid arts from passive ones. Ikishoten is passive and printed at level nine despite the current prerequisite. [Frenzied Warrior](https://finalfantasyd20.com/classes/base-classes/berserker/archetypes/frenzied-warrior/) confirms +6 Str and one extra full-attack strike. The Ogre now has an off-state that removes six Strength and restores four AC plus its separate non-frenzied weapon routine.

Fresh hydration and positive item/DC/resource checks, not native package certification. Individual qualifications below.

Printed HP 76, AC 22. Ki Pool 9 with four selections sharing charges, Sneak Attack 5d6, katana +11/+6 and shuriken +10/+5. Shell is a separate conditional defense.
