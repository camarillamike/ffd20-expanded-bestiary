# v0.1.46 group 8

Audited class-card details: Moonbeam is a 30-ft ranged touch for 1d6+4 ice with a separate Fort 18 blind rider, not save-only. Synastry is a move action, range 30, half healing to the bonded ally. Coat of Many Stars is an inactive +3 deflection toggle with nine hourly increments, not always-on AC. The Chanter grants 4d6 temporary HP on a move. Both underwater crossbows have the native underwater source ID and an action named Light Crossbow, matching both printed lines.

Fresh actor projections, including the companion. Positive checks are not native Foundry or package sign-off.

### [Megalodon](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/animals/shark-family/megalodon/)

Printed HP 136, AC 23. Bite +21, 17-20, bleed 1d10. Hydrotwister 6d6 water deals damage even on a successful Fort 21; that save negates the two statuses only. Reteethe has mutually exclusive +2/+4/+6/+8 Strength tiers, four rounds.

### [Merfolk Astrologist](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/humanoids/merfolk-family/merfolk-astrologist/)

Printed HP 67, AC 17. Draw 15, Lightspeed 7, Prescience 7, Synastry 4, Moonbeam Fort 18. The two printed typos Anitcipate Peril and Idenify resolve to real Anticipate Peril and Identify documents. Extra Draw has a separate official class-feat source. CL 9, concentration +13.

### [Merfolk Chanter](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/humanoids/merfolk-family/merfolk-chanter/)

Printed HP 76, AC 21. Shared 31-round bardic performance pool; Fascinate DC 19, Dissonance 9/day, Resonance 8/day. Perform (Harp) +17 and Perform (Sing) +24 are separate subskills, not universal skill modifiers.

### [Merfolk Informant](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/humanoids/merfolk-family/merfolk-informant/)

Printed HP 85, AC 24. Shared Ki 6. Katana +11/+6, off-hand wakizashi +9/+9 as printed, underwater crossbow +11/+6 is a native underwater weapon. The crossbow in the attack line lacks the parenthetical present in Gear.

### [Merfolk Terpsichorean](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/humanoids/merfolk-family/merfolk-terpsichorean/)

Printed HP 76, AC 23. Battle Dance 25 rounds/day; Ki 9; Wearying Dance DC 19 (misspelled Weavying). Rain and ordinary double-weapon routines have independent shaft rolls; do not combine 1d8+3 and 1d6+2 on a single strike.

### [Pale Head](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/undead/evil-head-family/pale-head/)

Printed HP 97, AC 20. Chill grants 13 rounds on activation (3/day); the separate Chill Hit rider deals 1d6 ice, Fort 14 vs stagger. Dust 2/day, Fort 16; Pumpkin Head is a +13 ranged touch with a missing-HP formula capped at ten d4s.

### [Psycheflayer](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/outsiders/soulflayer-family/psycheflayer/)

Printed HP 102, AC 20. Mind Blast is one target at 60 ft, 8d6 and Will 22. Time Slip has two independent DC 22 saves against aging and sleep, but only one daily charge. Page specifies +1 save DC for lightning/shadow; corrected Lighting spelling, split selected feats, apply the school bonus only once. Supplemental blue spells are explicitly modeled where absent from the spell pack.

### [Sahagin Black Mage](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/monstrous-humanoids/sahuagin-family/sahagin-black-mage/)

Printed HP 41, AC 14. Water Gun +7 Fort 19, one/day, distinct from spellbook damage. +4 to water spell damage only, including water orb; elemental shield 8/day, separate Mage Armor and Temper uses. CL 9, concentration +14, prepared MP includes the owned Extra MP only once.

### [Sahagin Scale Knight](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/monstrous-humanoids/sahuagin-family/sahagin-scale-knight/)

Printed HP 85, AC 20. Black Shark is a separate eight-d8 animal, HP 77, bite +13/+8 (2d6+10, 19-20). Its baseline Strength is 25. Reteethe starts off and caps at +4. The owner has 23 rage rounds, Animal Focus 9 min/day, Water Gun Fort 17.

### [Sahagin Sea Master](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/monstrous-humanoids/sahuagin-family/sahagin-sea-master/)

Printed HP 67, AC 17. Water Gun feat once/day is separate from the level-one spell. Analysis 6/day, Invoke and Scan Will 17, Light Rays three individual rays. CL 9 concentration +12, printed MP 41. The page's Monstruous Humanoid is normalized to monstrousHumanoid.
