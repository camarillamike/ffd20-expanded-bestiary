# v0.1.46 group 9

Lay on Hands: one eleven-use item has a standard ally heal, swift self heal, and no-save melee touch against undead. Innate spell items own their separate daily charges, reference real spell IDs, and spend zero spellbook MP.

Scarmiglione's hydrated Drain now uses the working expression `(min(5,floor(@cl/2)))d6`, native dark type, rather than the malformed compendium expression with its comma outside min. Group tests check this alternative explicitly. A 10th-level cast caps at 5d6.

Fresh original-page and hydrated-item comparison. Multiple attacks, setups, and on-hit riders are deliberately not collapsed into one damage roll.

### [Sahagin Vanguard](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/monstrous-humanoids/sahuagin-family/sahagin-vanguard/)

Printed HP 79, AC 22. Trident +15/+10, heavy crossbow +12/+7; Water Gun +12 Fort 17, water-only terrain defense, one daily blood frenzy. The source type typo is not a reason to replace monstrous humanoid with humanoid.

### [Sandragora](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/plants/grat-family/sandragora/)

Printed HP 123, AC 22. Four ranged seed rolls +16 for 1d6+2 each. Burst has fourteen separate +16 attacks for 1d6 per successful seed (maximum 14d6), Will 20 Confuse; no Strength on these burst dice. Cannon 2d6 at 60 feet.

### [Scarmiglione](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/bosses/archfiend-family/scarmiglione/)

Burst relocation Will 21; normal and empowered aura have distinct area, damage and retaliation rolls. The 2d6 backlash and 1d6 attack rider are not rolled by the relocation action. Power Attack is already included. CL 10, MP 50.
