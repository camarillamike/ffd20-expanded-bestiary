# v0.1.46 group 10

All five scoped test scripts also support `PACK046` set to the compiled .46 module directory. That mode reopens native packs read-only, requires exactly the expected number of owned documents per catalog entry, and runs the same routed checks against those native documents, without re-curating them.

The Zealot's five legacy finishers now have individual actions, including Haymaker without spending Stunning Fist uses and separate attacks against two targets for Twin Snakes. Names are sourced from the actor. Sequencing/rider details use the [archived legacy combo table](https://lucent-spire.fandom.com/wiki/Monk/Combo_Finishers), not the contemporary Fist of Rhalgr rewrite. This is a secondary rules witness; the current official actor does not print those details. The Interrogator's five finishers use the [official Kensai rules](https://finalfantasyd20.com/classes/hybrid-classes/samurai/archetypes/kensai/). Its No Fear is passive (+2 Will vs fear), and Magicide damages MP, not HP.

Fresh in-memory actor and companion tests; no release artifact generated or certified. Cross-actor costs and legacy features are called out individually.

### [Vanu Vanu Zealot](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/humanoids/vanu-vanu-family/vanu-vanu-zealot/)

Printed HP 103, AC 19. Stunning Fist +14, 2d4+4 (18-20), Fort 17, eleven/day. Flurry +13/+13/+8, Boost 7/day, shared Ki 7. The printed legacy combo list is not the modern Monk's chosen Blitz progression; do not certify missing legacy effects against a differently named modern feature.

Printed HP 67, AC 21. Performance 24 rounds, Dissonance 8/day DC 18, Resonance 7/day, inspiration 4d6 seven/day and Troubadour four/day. Perform (String) +20 and (Wind) +25. Printed Silence Verse resolves to Silent Verse.

Printed HP 67, AC 23. Two +1 Yagudo wakizashi, full routine +11/+9/+6/+4 (offsets -2,-5,-7), not two attacks at the same bonus. One 6-point Ki pool for four arts, one 5d6 sneak rider.

### [Yagudo Interrogator](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/humanoids/yagudo-family/yagudo-interrogator/)

Aerial Armor uses 4 summoner MP, Aero II uses 2; GM handles cross-actor transfer exactly once. Oracle has Blood Pact 4, Counter-Summon 7, Call 1 and shared Summon Monster 7.
