# v0.1.50: fresh-copy Foundry checks

Local compilation and pack verification are not a hosted Foundry playtest. Use Self Rolls, fresh world actors and unlinked tokens in an inactive QA scene. Do not change a production encounter or advance the world clock.

1. Setup: confirm v0.1.50 and count 989 actors across the 15 managed pack indexes without importing them. The catalog tracks 899 distinct entries. Verify the normal and mini-boss Dark Behemoths, Golbez's Shadow Dragon in Dragons, and all four Neo Exdeath parts.
2. MOAB: on fresh copies A and B, activate Fire only on A. Check that Detect Magic works on B without any template, Fire works on A, and a wrong-template cast on B posts no successful card and spends no MP. Turn on Dark and verify Dark IV is a ranged touch attack for 7d6 + casting modifier + min(CL,20) Shadow. Fire IX and Star Distortion are labeled reference only and must not spend MP or post a successful cast.
3. MOAB: cast one Spikes ward. The cast has no immediate damage; the separate triggered action should not spend MP again. Shock Spikes III deals 5d6 Lightning; Blaze Spikes III adds min(CL,20) to 5d6 Fire. Check Burn's repeat save and continuing damage separately after the initial cast. Do not apply these effects to live actors.
4. Raktavija: the shield starts active at 100. On a fresh unlinked QA token, check post-resistance elemental hits consume its own capacity, not a sibling's. While active it has DR 20/-; when depleted, DR 20 requires magic AND either adamantine or crystal to bypass. Test Fomoire similarly: active Murderous Might replaces base DR 20 with 15/-; the reductions must not add together.
5. Neo Exdeath: test a purely physical 30-point attack on Middle. Without all three of epic, good and chaotic, expect 15 HP loss; with all three, expect 30. Front, Top, Middle and Back have separate DR amounts (20, 20, 15, 10).
6. Obliterator Mk I and Mk II: active field 100, recovery 20 per round. Gamma: 140, recovery 28. Test a physical hit only after selecting the intended QA token; check damage remaining after defenses. Test regeneration, expiry and 24-hour restart only in a disposable world. Keep fire absorption distinct from field damage.

## Sources and limits

[MOAB spell chart](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1NbKtKkSfRS0gtSmbM0mw2pcTyAFcLG6xJTBi75H1W1c/edit) supplies the roster; the [FFd20 spell index](https://finalfantasyd20.com/magic/spells/) supplies individual rules. The [per-spell audit](development/batch-reviews/050/moab-spell-sources.json) lists **118 matched and 77 chart-only** spells. Chart-only entries retain their template and level, but have no automated action. None of these changes modifies player spell compendiums. Apply a rolled elemental template on the intended token; multiple templates are permitted by the chart. Handle GM choices, summon placement, suppression and external resource transfers at the table.

The [Fomoire statblock](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/outsiders/cieth-family/shambling-cieth/cieth-fomoire/) lists [Mighty Blow (Frog God Games)](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/feats/3rd-party-feats/frog-god-games/monster-feats-3pp-frog-god-games/mighty-blow/). At Huge it adds 10 feet to the knockback on a successful Awesome Blow (20 feet total), not to damage or CMB. Other adjudications and printed-stat discrepancies are listed by creature in the five group reviews. As of final integration the new compound DR, force fields, Raktavija's shield and listed elemental absorption profiles have runtime support; older per-group notes describe the isolated authoring stage, not the packaged module.
