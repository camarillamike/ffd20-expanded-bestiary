# FF D20 Expanded Bestiary v1.0.0

This stable release contains 1,052 actors from 944 source entries in 15 compendiums. It promotes the v0.1.52 content without changing actors, items, artwork, pack IDs, or gameplay scripts. No world migration, console command, or bulk import is needed.

## Verify the update in Foundry 13

1. From Setup, open **Add-on Modules** and update **FF D20 Expanded Bestiary** normally. Open the world and confirm **Manage Modules** shows **1.0.0** enabled.
2. Open the Expanded Bestiary compendium folder. Expect 15 packs and 1,052 actors across their indexes; the NPCs pack is intentionally empty.
3. Import a fresh **Malboro Menace (Melee)** from Expanded Plants into a QA folder. Use a Self Roll for **Vine x4** as a full attack. Expect four +24 attacks at 30 ft. with 2d6+10 Bludgeoning damage. No targets, damage application, active encounter, or world-clock change are needed.

The [v0.1.52 hosted checks](052-release-qa.md) passed for Machine's field, elemental Burst criticals, the melee and ranged Malboro Menace, and Neo Exdeath's three-part DR. Io's round regeneration and Bahamut Zero's phase timing remain deferred to a disposable world; they are not recorded as live passes.
