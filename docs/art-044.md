# Artwork for the third expansion

The compendium ships local portraits and uses the same local file for the prototype token. Official source images are preferred where available. The build converts animated GIF statblock portraits to a single PNG frame for Foundry. Neither importing an actor nor dragging a token needs the external website.

## Companions and forms

- The Centaur Ranger's wolf and Sahagin Tamer's shark reuse the corresponding local species art, not their owners' portraits.
- The Yagudo Mendicant's Valefor uses a separate wind avatar illustration.
- The ice Large Flan retains its published sprite. Seven companion portraits depict its Fire, Wind, Earth, Lightning, Water, Divine and Dark forms.
- The fire Bomb T2 (Balloon) retains the published sprite. The other eight selectable forms use portraits based on that silhouette in their own elemental palettes. These forms are packaged separately; creating one does not replace the original actor.
