# Source review: 0.1.38

The November 19, 2026 refresh read the official bestiary index and all 944 linked entries for the catalog. The build separately re-fetched the full pages for the 45 previously reviewed entries and the four new catalog entries. The wolf is a companion on its druid's page, not a fifth catalog entry. Review state is keyed by source URL because the bestiary includes two unrelated entries called Gorgon.

| Entry | Decisions checked against the source |
| --- | --- |
| [Cave Bat](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/magical-beasts/bat-family/cave-bat/) | Two magical-beast HD. The offense summary still calls the drain Blood Drain; the detailed Theft of Vitae ability is authoritative, not a second attack. Its failed Fortitude save drains once and heals from the same result. Screech staggers, excludes bats and uses a 20-foot burst. Critical Attack uses its own recharging bite. |
| [Centaur Archer](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/monstrous-humanoids/centaur-family/centaur-archer/) | Archer 1, not racial HD. Medium longbow and longspear, Large armor, 20 arrows and paired secondary hooves. The listed longbow bonus is the baseline; Aim and Point-Blank Shot are optional native roll modifiers. The separate Aim skill buff affects only checks the GM makes against the designated opponent. |
| [Centaur Forager](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/monstrous-humanoids/centaur-family/centaur-forager/) | Druid 1, 4 MP, Wisdom primary book: four at-will cantrips and three 1st-level spells. CL 1 with concentration +4, +8 defensively. Nature Sense supplies the two skill bonuses once. The regular club and enhanced Shillelagh attack are alternatives. Five throwable shortspears and a light wooden shield (a shield type, not Small-size gear). Nature Bond points to the separate wolf. |
| Forager's Wolf (same source) | No independent CR/XP printed. One companion d10 with full BAB, not an ordinary animal d8. Bite with a separate free trip; Crunch is a swift additional bite with a 1d4-round recharge. Record the bleed on the victim over two rounds, not as extra immediate HP damage. Link and Shared Senses apply to its handler. |
| [Centaur Striker](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/monstrous-humanoids/centaur-family/centaur-striker/) | One authored Lancer archetype level, preserving the printed saves. Mounted Combat and Skill Focus (Ride) are bonus feats. The real Shaft Trip talent supplies Improved Trip. The halberd mounted-charge option adds the extra die once; a regular halberd, thrown spear and two hooves are separate routines. Do not assume every centaur attack qualifies as a mounted charge. |

Rules cross-checks: [Archer: Aim and Archery Style](https://finalfantasyd20.com/classes/core-classes/archer/), [Lancer: Mounted Charge and Skilled Rider](https://finalfantasyd20.com/classes/core-classes/dragoon/archetypes/lancer/), [Dragoon Talents: Shaft Trip](https://finalfantasyd20.com/classes/core-classes/dragoon/dragoon-talents/), [companion progression, Link and Shared Senses](https://finalfantasyd20.com/classes/core-classes/beastmaster/), and [Shillelagh](https://finalfantasyd20.com/magic/spells/s/shillelagh/).

**Previously reviewed pages:** Minor Bat now uses the new Theft of Vitae name but retains the requested static DC 11. Baby Tonberry's revised knife is 1d2+4, using Dexterity to damage on both the knife and its feature action. The other 43 full-page statblock text extracts were unchanged.

**Art:** Cave Bat, Centaur Archer and Centaur Striker use cached source artwork. The Forager page has no illustration for either actor. Original, transparent portraits were generated for the club-and-shield centaur gathering herbs and its grey woodland wolf; both are packaged locally for avatar and token use.

**Verification boundary:** The offline import test reads temporary copies of the real FFd20 system and FFd20 Content item databases, then runs the module importer against seven actors. It checks spell hydration, selected class features, gear sizes and quantities, paired hooves, the companion's actions and both revised existing actors. It does not simulate the Foundry client, a running combat, or the other modules installed in a particular world.
