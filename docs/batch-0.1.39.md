# Expansion 0.1.39

Five groups of ten new catalog pages were reviewed against the November 19 catalog refresh. Counts refer to source URLs, not the number of actor documents: the 99 covered entries yield 107 actors with the earlier bonded companion and seven additional Flan forms. The canonical Small Flan is Fire. Each of the eight forms has its own defenses, slam damage and one learned spell.

The URL-keyed ledger is in `expansion-progress.json`. The per-actor import run, including which installed pack supplied each lookup, is in `import-039.json`. The check uses real item documents from copies of the local FFD20 system and FFD20 Content packs. It runs the packaged importer with a simulated Foundry host, not a live server; actor preparation, token rendering, dice dialogs, shared resources and chat-card clicks still merit live testing.

## Rulings and limitations

The pre-push recheck covers all 107 packaged actors, not just the expansion. The report includes 18 optional world-actor creations (nine forms for each bomb); these do not increase the pack count. Checks include published normal, touch and flat-footed AC, saves, HP, CMD, spell selections, and the actual items after compendium lookup. No live Foundry session was used.

- The [Vanu Vanu Swiftblade](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/humanoids/vanu-vanu-family/vanu-vanu-swiftblade/) is a 1st-level Dancer but prints 61 HP derived from seven dice. Its class level stays at one and its class HP contribution reproduces 61. It does not gain six additional levels of features.
- Merfolk Priest retains the unusual printed spell DCs 16 and 17. The adjustment is on its book, not on every merfolk. Seasinger's DC bonus is conditional.
- On a normal full Apply Damage click, packaged percentage resistances halve only qualifying damage left after native defenses; 50% never means 50 points. Shift-Apply is still the native manual dialog. Cie'th DR 5 is bypassed by either adamantine or crystal: mark the weapon with the actual material, not just a name. Elemental affinities follow the [FFd20 elements rules](https://finalfantasyd20.com/magic/elements/).
- Bombino and Cherry Bomb default to Fire. As GM, open either sheet and use **Create Bomb Form** to make a fresh world actor in another element without editing the source. Only Bombino receives the chosen form's spell. Roll Light Fortification on a qualifying critical or sneak attack; it is not an immunity.
- The two 1st-level monk statblocks list Blitz Techniques without a selection. They include the real Monk rules reference, not a new attack or ki pool. The [current rules](https://finalfantasyd20.com/classes/core-classes/monk/blitz-techniques/) grant the first selection at 3rd level. Their printed Flurry and Stunning Fist are usable separately.
- Duelist Stance and the monk/dancer Unarmored Defense buffs start active for the published loadouts. Turn them off when the character no longer qualifies. Kobold Watch's Chosen Weapon is scoped to its spear, not its sling; Orcish Fodder's falchion uses two-handed Strength and the scaling Warrior bonus.
- Aquatic, airborne or terrain-dependent modifiers and shell withdrawal are conditional. Enable the appropriate buff only when its trigger applies; notes-only AC, DR and concealment modifiers require GM adjudication. Spell or item use does not automatically set an opposing token's conditions or deduct a master's resources.
- Ammes's Gravity removes half the **target's current HP**, rounded down, after a failed save; the GM applies that loss. Skull Warrior's Marrow Drain affects MP instead of HP; heal it for the amount actually drained. Poison and disease that damage attributes are not treated as HP damage.
- The Minstrel's four performance modes use one six-round pool. Deeds draw from the Sea Watch's two panache points. Track the shared pool on the owning feature. Sea Watch uses the [legacy Fencer deeds](https://www.finalfantasyd20.com/pdf/Fencer.pdf) reflected by its entry.
- The printed Kojin Guard and Orcish Grappler reference legacy combo finishers. The Guard gets a separate [Art of the Sword](https://finalfantasyd20.com/classes/hybrid-classes/samurai/archetypes/kensai/) line action. Dragon Kick has a separate unarmed strike; the Grappler's page does not give its original prerequisite sequence. Do not substitute the current, higher-cost archetype ability for it.
- [Leaf](https://finalfantasyd20.com/magic/spells/l/leaf/) was not in either installed spell pack. It has an authored Primary spell with separate spring, summer, autumn, winter, and seasonless attack actions. The Mesmerizer's scroll has no named spell on its page and remains unassigned treasure.
- Seven entries without source illustrations have original, locally packaged portraits used for both avatar and prototype token. Other new entries use cached artwork, not external token URLs.
