# Credits & sources

The creature statblocks and rules are adapted from the [Final Fantasy d20 Bestiary](https://www.finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/). The module uses FF d20 system and content compendiums, and Pathfinder content where applicable. The [upstream notices](../notices/) and [per-actor source records](../source/curation/) are retained for attribution and reference.

## Artwork

Portraits and tokens include illustrations from the source bestiary and content packs, as well as AI-generated illustrations for creatures without suitable art and AI-assisted variants of existing portraits. For example, the seven Mooglesguard portraits were AI-generated; they are not official Final Fantasy artwork. The [art records](art-046.json) and [elemental bomb art notes](bomb-art.md) document examples of the different sources and methods.

## Development

AI was also used to analyze statblocks, author Foundry actions and descriptive text, write code, and develop validation tests. The original tabletop rules are the work of their respective authors. Source comparisons and Foundry play tests are recorded separately: an offline check is not a claim that an action has been tested in a live game.
