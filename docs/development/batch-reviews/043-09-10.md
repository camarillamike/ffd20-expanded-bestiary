**Screech cooldown verification.** Refreshed the Blood Bat source-to-hydrated diagnostic: Screech, and no other item on this actor, carries `flags.ffd20-bestiary-builder.dailyRoundCooldown = "1d4"`. The item has two daily uses, costs one, and has no native recharge formula. The effect card marks exactly one interval roll; the separate 1d3 roll is for stagger. In the local FFd20 source, `ActionUse.process` calls `pf1PreActionUse` after enriching effect notes and honors synchronous `false` before the native debit and chat. The deadline belongs to the owned item, not the base actor or another unlinked token. The same encounter uses the due turn; outside it, advance Foundry world time for the remaining interval. Expiry never replenishes a daily use.
