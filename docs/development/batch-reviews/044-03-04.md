## G4 verifier handoff

* **Pixie, `spell.light.action DC`: documented, not a literal match.** Its page prints a single first-level DC 14 and separately lists Light Focus; it does not print a specific DC 15 next to Light. The [feat](https://finalfantasyd20.com/feats/general-feats/light-focus/) adds one to saves for light spells. [Light](https://finalfantasyd20.com/magic/spells/l/light/) has a Fortitude save to prevent Illuminated, not to avoid its on-hit damage. Book DC 14 + one owned-action adjustment = 15. The hydrated feat supplies no numerical change, the active global/school DC sum is zero, and no other saving spell has the +1. The regression now requires the shared verifier's actual `documented` row with printed 14 / actual 15; it no longer waives a discrepancy.
* **Merfolk Seer, `feat.Extra Draw.provenance`: published feat, locally represented.** The [official class feat table](https://finalfantasyd20.com/feats/class-feats/) lists Draw as prerequisite and two additional uses as the benefit. The [Astrologian Draw rule](https://finalfantasyd20.com/classes/base-classes/astrologian/#draw) yields 4 + 3 = 7 before the feat, hence 9 under the general rules. The creature explicitly lists both Extra Draw and Draw 7/day. The sheet keeps the creature-specific 7, records the conflict on the feat, and gives the feat neither a second pool nor a modifying change. The published feat now has `sourceUrl` for the authoritative table and `actorSourceUrl` for the creature; the test verifies the index has no same-name feat, there is no fabricated compendium ID, and the single actual Draw pool is 7/day. The generic index-only provenance row cannot establish this; classify it as documented local provenance, not a compendium match.
* **Kojin Tactician, `skill.Knowledge (Geograpy)`: source typo, not a second skill.** Keep the literal source spelling in evidence. The interpreted shared-verifier row is Knowledge (Geography) +10, and Knowledge (Planes) independently matches +10. On the sheet, native `kge` and `kpl` each have 4 ranks + 3 Int + 3 trained class skill, with no residual adjustment. The regression requires both actual numeric comparisons. The literal lookup remains unsupported; it must not generate a synthetic skill or second bonus.
