# Expansion 044: groups 05 and 06
Valefor uses the native Outsider type and pack, with Avatar retained as the companion's racial HD class, per the [Summoner avatar rules](https://finalfantasyd20.com/classes/base-classes/summoner/). Its master's Energy Beam and Terra Homing deal non-elemental and holy damage, respectively; the owned spell actions supply those types explicitly.

## Group 05

- [quacho](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/magical-beasts/quacho-family/quacho/): Quacho: 59 HP; bite +8 and a separate bite counter on 25%. Chill carries ice damage and DC 13 Fort only against staggering. Aqua Orb uses the real Elemental Orb with the water choice. Playable racial MP is 8, not the printed 10.
- [red-cap](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/humanoids/goblin-family/red-cap/): Red Cap: Warrior 5; masterwork longsword and composite bow (+4) have separate real weapon documents; equipped breastplate, shield, and melee-only shield bonus account for AC 22. Engulfing Winds 3/day, DC 14. The warrior selects Disarm for Combat Defenses, though the CMD line instead calls out trip. Both source statements retained.
- [red-chocobo](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/magical-beasts/chocobo-family/red-chocobo/): Red Chocobo: bite and two talons have 10-foot reach; Choco Cure 3d6+HD and Choco Flame ranged touch are independent 2/day abilities. The DC 13 Reflex save negates Burning, not damage.
- [redlegs](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/vermin/centipede-family/redlegs/): Redlegs: bite and sting each deal piercing plus noncritical fire; Burn has DC 16 and separate ongoing and retaliation damage. Critical Attack multiplies the physical roll, not the separate fire die; recharge 1d3 rounds.
- [remorazz](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/vermin/centipede-family/remorazz/): Remorazz: both strikes add ice, not Redlegs' Burn. Critical Attack has a 1d3 recharge. Earth 5, ice 10, and strong wind (50%) coexist on the same actor.
- [rokh](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/magical-beasts/vulture-family/rokh/): Rokh: three independent simultaneous ranged touch rays, one daily use; Black Cloud (DC 15) and Blinder (DC 16) have separate blind durations. +4 vs disease only, not a universal save bonus.
- [sahagin-apprentice](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/monstrous-humanoids/sahuagin-family/sahagin-apprentice/): Sahagin Apprentice: Black Mage 4, 19 real spells, six Elemental Orb choices, water specialty applied to Water, Water II, Engulf's recurring damage, the water orb, and the rod. Read 'Tough of Fatigue' as Touch of Fatigue. Base MP 6 + Int tiers 3 + real Extra MP 4 = 13. Both metamagic talents have their own pools; Water Gun DC 16.
- [sahagin-chemist](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/monstrous-humanoids/sahuagin-family/sahagin-chemist/): Sahagin Chemist: Chemist 4, two separate bite routines, aquatic dodge separate from frenzy. Seven daily bomb infusions vs three physical catalysts. Fire and Acid Bomb share the counter; DC 15 applies only to splash. The [FFd20 Chemist discovery](https://www.finalfantasyd20.com/pdf/Chemist.pdf) changes Acid Bomb to earth damage on the hit and 1d6 non-elemental one round later. The delayed roll does not consume a second infusion. Real trident, leather armor, cures and discoveries.
- [sahagin-tamer](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/monstrous-humanoids/sahuagin-family/sahagin-tamer/): Sahagin Tamer: published 4d12 and full BAB retained; aquatic AC, blood frenzy and class rage separate. Shark is an additional owned actor with aquatic movement, separate HP 30, grab, swallow damage and two mutually exclusive four-round Reteethe stages. Page mislabels the critical-hit ability as Keen Scent. Large size retained; its grid footprint is the separately printed 15 ft. Both sheets have all twelve focus states; only the tamer expends daily minutes. Printed hide is hide armor.
- [security-eye](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/constructs/magitek-family/scouter-bot/security-eye/): Security Eye: 42 body HP and separate 25-point field; fast healing 5 applies only to an active field, not body HP. Both integrated lasers are real tech weapons with touch attacks; torch has peculiar tool reach/hardness rules. Flash DC 16 and conditional camouflage are separate. Field-dependent critical immunity is not granted permanently. With the field down, a damaging critical invokes the [machina rule](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/universal-monster-rules/creature-properties/): fixed Fort DC 12, stunned on failure or staggered on success for one round.

Local check: `node tools/test-044-05-06.mjs 05`, 11 hydrated actors including the shark. The test uses the cached real compendium documents, independently projects HP, saves and all three ACs, and checks actor-specific mechanics. This is not a live tabletop session.

## Group 06

- [siren-ffv-alive](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/bosses/siren-ff5-forms/siren-ff5-alive/): Two real weapon documents, 20 real spells, SR 15 and eight elemental resistances 5. Swift self-heal and hostile touch share seven Transfer uses; a single 3d4 roll is split holy/shadow. Pulse separates allies' 2d8 healing from enemies' mixed 4d4 (Reflex DC 17 half). Wisdom-based racial base 8 + tier bonus 7 + Extra MP 5 = 20; page prints 17. Immediate Undead Transform recharges in 1d2+1 rounds; switch sheets, do not transfer HP loss.
- [siren-ffv-undead](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/bosses/siren-ff5-forms/siren-ff5-undead/): Separate undead HP bar (84), AC 18/13/15, DR 5/- and fire weakness. Shadow staff +6 vs touch. Bloodstrikes grants a +6 draining claw in addition to normal attacks, not a permanent full-attack change. Critical Attack uses the explicitly smaller 1d6 base; Venomous Clasp deals 2d6+5, Fort DC 15 vs poison only. Black Aura has separate round and melee-retaliation rolls. Hume Transform preserves both pools of HP.
- [snow-crab](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/vermin/crab-family/snow-crab/): AC 16/9/16, HP 84 (the bracketed 120 is not added), Large claws +9 with grab +18 and 1d6+10 constrict. Giant Blade is one 2d6+20 claw, expressly without grab. Gaze DC 15 and 1d4+1 recharge; Icy Foam and Tornado each make a +2 touch attack at 35 ft and independently have 3/day. Out-of-water limit is 18 hours.
- [sparna](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/monstrous-humanoids/garuda-family/sparna/): AC 18/15/13, CMD 27, Cha-based Aero II, 11 MP. Iron Nail now inherits the actual +8 bite; multiplies the physical roll once, on hit imposes -2 AC on the target for 1d3+1 rounds. Cooldown 1d4. The armor penalty is off on the attacker by default.
- [ultros-river](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/bosses/ultros-family/ultros-river/): 126 HP, water absorption, ice resistance 10, fire/lightning weaknesses. Bite 10 ft, four tentacles at 20 ft; grab +16, separate constrict roll. Frenzy adds two tentacle attacks for two rounds, even without a full attack. Acid Droplet actually deals water: Fort DC 16 negates only poison, unlike Ink's attack and DC 18 blindness; separate 1d2/1d4 recharges. Jet is a 250-ft straight full-round move.
- [vanu-vanu-pickpocket](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/humanoids/vanu-vanu-family/vanu-vanu-pickpocket/): Corrected 'Theif' to real Thief 4; one shortsword, five daggers, studded leather. Paired +4/+4, solo shortsword +6 and thrown dagger +6 are distinct. Real Surprise Attack, Throw Off-Balance (1/day), Mark, Measure the Mark, and 2d6 precision damage with three injury choices. Replaced the reference Trapfinding item's broken formula; its Perception bonus applies to traps only. Printed AC includes one more point than ordinary studded leather and an unlisted dodge point: adjustments are labeled.
- [vanu-vanu-red-mage](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/humanoids/vanu-vanu-family/vanu-vanu-red-mage/): Real Red Mage 4: 5 base + 3 Charisma tiers = 8 MP, 14 spells. Read 'sling (10)' as a sling and 10 bullets. Spell Combat and Spellstrike are weapon actions; one 4-point arcane pool for weapon enhancement or immediate Spell Shield. Quick Cast retains its printed 1/day pool. The selected Red Magery item spends its own 1/day Extra Quick Cast charge instead; this is two total uses, not three; early Convert is explicitly on this NPC. Longsword focus included in listed routine without adding a global bonus.
- [vanu-vanu-trainee](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/humanoids/vanu-vanu-family/vanu-vanu-trainee/): The senses header prints Perception +10, while Skills prints +11. The sheet note adopts Skills +11 before situational bonuses. Real Monk 4; source equipment includes a +1 resistance cloak but its saves already include it. Equipped projection is Fort/Ref/Will 8/7/5. Contradictory AC breakdown reconciled to published 17/13/12. Distinct +6/+6 flurry, DC 15 Stunning Fist 4/day, shared 5-point ki pool and Chakra healing. The NPC specifically calls for legacy Beat Rush and Twin Snakes; those have distinct sequences, targets and rolls. Six attack substitutions build the two finishers without expending ki; see the [legacy Monk class text](https://www.scribd.com/document/667104306/Monk). Race's ranged-only defense and bonuses vs flying foes are not permanent.
- [wiggly-hopper](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/outsiders/hedgehog-pie-family/wiggly-hopper/): 5-HD racial caster, 11 MP, four real spells. Full-round Scavenge rolls Survival; 15 or lower poison, 16-25 heal 1d6+7, 26+ unlock a single Chain Spell for one minute. Chain begins empty; its three spells still cost MP. Rolled Up is an optional +3 natural armor enhancement and only then do grapple attempts trigger 1d6 spines. Printed 18/14/15 AC is preserved with touch-only and flat-footed-only changes.
- [yagudo-mendicant](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/humanoids/yagudo-family/yagudo-mendicant/): Real Summoner 4, 10 real spells, base 6 + Charisma tiers 3 = 9 MP vs printed 8, with GM note. Real bracers +3 and labeled -1 armor correction match the +2 AC breakdown. Primary and secondary desperate bites, shared Summon Monster I/II 6/day, Gear 6/day, Blood Pact 3/day as printed. Energy Beam has Reflex half (DC 14); Magic Shield has harmless Will negates (DC 15); Terra Homing has ranged touch and Fortitude partial (DC 15). The page's 'Knowledge (arcane) +8' is Knowledge (Arcana): 4 ranks + 1 Int + 3 class skill. The class note records that spelling; the verifier independently matches +8 on its canonical check. Separate Valefor: BAB +3 from 3 full-progression HD; no leftover Creature BAB adjustment. 3d10, 28 HP, +7 claws/bite, wind immunity, 60-ft flight; 14 Str/Con with a separate active 18-score state matching the parenthesized combat values (the page gives no cause). Augment Summoning explicitly excludes the avatar. Printed touch 15 and flat-footed 19 do not follow the components; labeled adjustments apply to those modes only. Wind Claw has a single extra wind die and a 1d4+1 recharge. Aero II costs 2 MP, never charged against both sheets.
