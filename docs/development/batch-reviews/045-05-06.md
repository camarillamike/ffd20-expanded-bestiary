## Checkpoint 100: independent exceptions

Read the 100-page JSON in source-preview mode (nativeVerified false). Group 5 then had ten unsupported and one discrepancy; group 6 had seven unsupported. These are not claims about the native package.

- Lamia: the Melee line explicitly says Wisdom drain. The hydrated attack `Touch.system.actions[0]` has touch enabled, +11, `formula: "1d4"`, and empty HP damage parts by design. Knowledge (local) fills the free choice, not arcana twice. The class has a skill.klo choice note and skill.acr Jump +9; the source ordinary Acrobatics is +5.
- Malboro: commas inside square brackets are not routine separators. Range actor: attack Spit +8, 1d4+6, 30 ft, Fort 18 negates paralysis for 1d4 rounds only; attack Vine Tendril x2 +8/+8, 100 ft, grab/pull/Feed, no initial HP damage, Feed limited 1/day. Melee actor: the same Spit; Vine x2 +6/+6, 20 ft, 1d4+6, grab/feed upon one Medium prey per round.
- Cursemaker and Fighter: hydrated class contextNotes skill.acr specify Jump +4 and +3 respectively. Serjeant: skill.acr Jump +11, skill.sur Track +10. These are not replacements for ordinary Acrobatics or Survival.
- Nakk: source omits a semicolon between Survival and Racial Modifiers. Companion character has Survival +3, context skill.sur scent-tracking +7; the class carries three independent type:racial, formula:2 changes targeting skill.per, skill.ste and skill.sur.
- Missing combat feat pack records: hydrated feat flags ffd20-bestiary-builder.review045Semantic are metal-cactuar:Sidestep, old-quadav:Stalwart, orcish-serjeant:Hamatulatsu. [Sidestep](https://legacy.aonprd.com/advancedplayersguide/advancedFeats.html) has a conditional immediate 5-ft step. [Stalwart](https://legacy.aonprd.com/ultimateCombat/ultimateCombatFeats.html) exchanges the qualifying dodge bonus for DR, capped at five. [Hamatulatsu](https://www.aonprd.com/FeatDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Hamatulatsu) can change unarmed damage to piercing; the critical rider is automatic with no save. The initial local version had an incorrect save; corrected after checking the feat directly.
