# v0.1.46 group 18

Ten hydrated actor action fixtures. Native package and cross-actor audit not yet tested.

## kotto

[Canonical source](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/monstrous-humanoids/wendigo-family/kotto/). AC 23, touch 13, flat-footed 19 (+4 dex, +10 natural, -1 size); hp 176 (14d10+112). Melee 4 Slams +24 (2d6+11)

Four slams and two rocks, 120-ft increments. Blood Rage next turn after being hit; Raise Dukes 1/day under half HP. Conditional counters and rooting are qualified rather than passive AC.

## leanan

[Canonical source](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/fey/leanan-family/leanan/). AC 23, touch 18, flat-footed 18 (+3 deflect, +4 dex, +1 dodge, +5 natural); hp 99 [144] (18d6+36). Melee Slam +9 (1d6 plus burn) or Touch +9 (1d2 Wis damage)

Lava Dome detailed description (30 ft./Will 23) takes precedence over contradictory header (40 ft./21). Healing Flame splits one roll; heal only on failed Fort save. Touch applies Wisdom damage, not HP damage.

## lich

[Canonical source](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/bosses/archfiend-family/lich/). AC 25, touch 21, flat-footed 17 (+4 natural, +7 deflection, +5 Dex, -1 size); hp 300 [432] (12d12+72). Melee 2 claws +18 (2d6+6 plus 1d6 earth damage)

12 HD; separate DC 25 Doom strike, Death Grip (critical outcome changes durations), DC 23 Earthquake and Filth Cloud, and HD-divisible-by-five death burst. Outdoor Comet/Cometra impacts use earth only. Icy Spikes II normalized to Ice Spikes II.

## magma-tortoise

[Canonical source](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/magical-beasts/turtle-family/magma-tortoise/). AC 29, touch 7, flat-footed 29 (-1 dex, +22 natural, -2 size); hp 156 [224] (14d10+84). Melee 2 Bites +23 (2d8+10)

Earth immune but fire resistant 10, not fire immune. Critical Attack 1d2, 8d6 fire breath 1d4+1. Two mutually exclusive shell modes; no automatic cumulative defense.

## mega-armor

## sharnbulette
