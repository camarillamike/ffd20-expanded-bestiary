# v0.1.46 group 19

Ten source pages, eleven freshly hydrated actor fixtures including Dapper Decoy. Native candidate not yet tested.

## sir-bearington

[Canonical source](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/bosses/sir-bearington/). AC 26, touch 14, flat-footed 21 (+5 Dex, +12 natural, -1 size); hp 406 [532] (14d10+126). Melee Sword cane +24/+19/+14 (1d8+10) or 2 Claws +23 (1d6+10 plus grab), Bite +23 (1d8+10)

Two actor documents share art. Dapper Decoy lacks main-only specials; set current HP to original on summon, remains ten rounds. Three phase healing and resource behavior, two Grizzly Rend weapon modes, independent phase-III Berserk save.

## stone-golem

## tantala

5/day Breath Wing, unlimited Zombie Breath; airborne Helldive +24 and DC 23 stun. Check 10% disease exposure only once per target for this individual attacker.

## ultros-cave

[Canonical source](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/bosses/ultros-family/ultros-cave/). AC 29, touch 13, flat-footed 19 (+6 Dex, +14 natural, -1 size); hp 435 [570] (15d10+135). Melee Bite +24 (1d8+9), 8 Tentacles +21 (1d8+4 plus grab)

Four extra tentacles for four rounds at HD 15; 20-foot reach. Snowstorm and Level 3 Confuse both track two uses per day plus four-sided cooldown, not unlimited charges. Header perception and skills differ; printed attacks retained.

## white-cait

[Canonical source](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/fey/cait-sith-family/white-cait/). AC 22, touch 15, flat-footed 17 (+4 Dex, +7 natural, +1 dodge); hp 99 [144] (18d6+36). Melee +1 Quarterstaff +10 (1d6+1)

Healing Arts applies to healing-school spells: CL 18 to 22, four additional same-size healing dice for dice-based HP healing, plus four to existing timed durations. Extra undead-only SR check bonus is qualified. Actual stock spell records embedded so hydration does not discard the individual CL offset. No offensive or enhancement spell receives it. Greedy Feedback and Pay to Play are separate.
