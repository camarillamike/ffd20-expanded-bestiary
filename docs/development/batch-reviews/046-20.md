# v0.1.46 group 20

Ten refreshed individual pages and ten hydrated actor fixtures. Native candidate not yet tested. Conditional barrier affinity arrays are attached to individual OFF-by-default buffs; a GM must apply their conditional defenses unless runtime support is supplied.

## dark-elemental

[Canonical source](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/outsiders/elemental-family/shadow-elemental/dark-elemental/). AC 30, touch 21, flat-footed 19 (+10 Dex, +1 dodge, +9 natural); hp 136 [208] (16d10+48). Melee 2 slams +26 (1d6+2 plus 2d8 shadow damage)

DR 10/-, shadow absorption, restricted Elemental Orb and four barrier states. White Wind 1/day, 20 ft., excludes caster, min(current HP, 10 x HD). Barrier state data supplied for conditional damage integration.

## death-manticore

[Canonical source](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/magical-beasts/manticore-family/death-manticore/). AC 23, touch 11, flat-footed 20 (+3 Dex, +12 natural, -2 size); hp 187 [225] (15d10+105). Melee bite +22 (2d6+9), 2 claws +22 (3d4+9 plus grab), tail slap +17 (2d6+4)

Deadly Hold combines one claw damage roll and its additional 3d4. Two different charging tails, DC 20 sandstorm with separate wind and earth rolls, and HD-multiple-of-five death effect.

## draugar

[Canonical source](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/undead/skeleton-family/draugar/). AC 31, touch 14, flat-footed 28 (+8 armor, +3 dex, +9 natural, +1 deflection); hp 142 (17d8+68). Melee +1 Greatsword +21/+16/+11 (2d6+11)

Greatsword iteratives and real armor/ring. Blood Saber and Malediction distinguish failed saves (half actual damage healed) from successful saves (half damage, no healing). Hell Slash charges once and discharges on the next hit.

## forgiven-enmity

[Canonical source](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/outsiders/sin-eater-family/forgiven-enmity/). AC 29, touch 13; flat-footed 24 (+5 Dex, +16 natural, -2 size); hp 232 [300] (15d10+150). Melee Bite +23 (2d6+10), Gore +23 (1d8+10), 2 Claws +23 (1d6+10), Tail Slap +21 (2d6+5)

Aqualung uses Reflex here (not generic Fortitude), Fulmination Ref 22, three separate recharges. Tail Swing Fort 27 and pushes 30 ft.; routine tail has 20-ft reach.

## gespenst

[Canonical source](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/undead/reaper-family/gespenst/). AC 22, touch 14, flat-footed 18 (+4 Dex, +8 natural); hp 127 (15d8+60). Melee 2 scythe claws +14 (2d4+3/×4) or flurry +12/+12/+12 (2d4+3/x4)

Three -2 flurry attacks, x4 critical, normal claws remain distinct. Rend Armor targets worn armor only, not creature HP. Lvl. 5 Death and Death Cutter DC 20.

## gorgias

[Canonical source](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/magical-beasts/mammoth-family/gorgias/). AC 24, touch 9, flat-footed 23 (+1 dex, +15 natural, -2 size); hp 183 (15d10+112). Melee Gore +23 (2d8+12), Slam +23 (1d8+12), 2 Stomps +21 (2d6+6)

Snowstorm 2/day, 30-ft line, 14d6, Ref 17; Trample 1d8+18, DC 29. Improved Natural Attack applies to gore.

## gorgimera

[Canonical source](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/magical-beasts/chimera-family/gorgimera/). AC 26, touch 10; flat-footed 24 (+2 dex, +16 natural, -2 size); hp 161 (15d10+80). Melee Bite +20 (2d6+7), Bite +20 (1d8+7 plus burn), Gore +20 (1d8+7), 2 Claws +20 (1d6+7)

Only smaller bite burns. Three independently qualified elemental attacks, including Ref 20 Aqualung, Ref 21 Fulmination; snowstorm not daily-limited.

## hell-house

[Canonical source](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/bosses/hell-house/). AC 24, touch 12, flat-footed 20 (+3 Dex, +12 natural, -2 size, +1 dodge); hp 256 [400] (16d10+40). Melee Slam +24 (2d6+15) [Stage I, II, and III] or 3 Claws +24 (1d8+10), Pick +24 (2d8+10 piercing/x4) [Stage II and III]

Eight exclusive Barrier Shift states, disabled during stage II. Charged abilities are shared between normal and enhanced actions. Suck-In uses detailed 4d6/6d6 per stage rather than the overview's 5d6. Physical damage replacement and resistance are conditional; no unconditional reduction. Separate repairs, salvos, rays, eight-claw Rush, and two-round aerial bombardment.

## ice-golem

[Canonical source](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/constructs/golem-family/ice-golem/). AC 26, touch 8; flat-footed 26 (-1 dex, +18 natural, -1 size); hp 118 (16d10+30). Melee 2 Slams +25 (2d10+10 plus 1d6 ice damage and slow)

Cold on contact and Chill from imbuing a weapon are separate. Hailstorm 3/day creates eight damage instances with repeat rolls that do not spend another charge; DC 20, range 100. Destruction has two damage types. No printed Chill DC; explicitly derived supernatural DC 23.

## inferno-golem

[Canonical source](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/constructs/golem-family/inferno-golem/). AC 26, touch 8; flat-footed 26 (-1 dex, +18 natural, -1 size); hp 118 (16d10+30). Melee 2 Slams +25 (2d10+10 plus 1d6 fire damage and slow)

Megaflash full 12d6 damage; Fort only avoids Blind. Red Hot only after taking damage from a melee critical; immediate slam. Haste after first combat round.
