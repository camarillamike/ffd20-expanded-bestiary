# Group 1: v0.1.49

All ten source pages were read with pageFields. The group draft builds and hydrates ten actors without unresolved item lookups. This is builder/output verification, not a live Foundry test. No shared pack was rebuilt.

| Source | Encounter handling and remaining manual steps |
| --- | --- |
| Greater Rathalos | Choose wings on the ground or venomous claws in the air; tail and bite are common to both routines. Rake is separate. The advancement paragraph is not another CR 15 form. Fire Roar uses the described cone; its text also calls it a burst. |
| Hades | Black Cauldron has independent damage, ten status saves, and self-healing. Select phases, position walls/strands, operate chain orbs and summon the three waves manually. Bone Shield prevention is tracked on the recipient. The printed phase-II trigger says both 40% and 462 HP; they disagree. Dual Strike 2.0 distributes attacks according to targets, not a fixed full attack. |
| Hashmal | Separate equipped scythes and attack routines have four and two swings. Both hands have Landwaster modes. The printed defensive Dex contribution is +3, not the +7 modifier used for initiative and Reflex. |
| Ifrit | Select encounter phase, Flaming Fury, and whether Sear occurred last round. Four aura modes distinguish normal and post-Sear damage; persistent plume damage is separate from impact. Manually place puddles and apply pushback. |
| Ixion | Aerospark changes action cost across three phases. Delay Buster's initiative penalty is a recipient buff; initiative reordering and end-of-round removal are GM decisions. Water resistance 10 and half resistance are both present. |
| Judge Master | Three daily pools, determination choices, moderate fortification, judgment choices, +3 sword and +2 armor/shield. Equipped AC is 31/15/26. Stalwart is the Knight class ability. Apply Damage includes DR 9/- only with armor equipped, not a shield alone; both states were checked offline. Judgment stacking, shield redirection and badge removal need adjudication. |
| Jura Aevis | Alternate Form links the existing Alte Roite compendium actor. Replace the token/statblock manually, retaining damage and clearing negative effects on reversion. Maelstrom requires the target's current HP and HD; it is not ordinary damage. |
| Kirin | Spellbook and phase-specific ray counts, Hallowed Armor retaliation rolls and Revered Weapon effects. Healing Master uses the two separately chosen spells; Bypass Spell Resistance is tracked per target, not as a single daily charge. Unicorn Purification says all bad statuses, despite referencing single-effect Basuna. |
| Kraken | Separate four- and eight-tentacle routines, grapple and throw modes, water absorption, DR 15/chaotic AND silver. During Lifespring use 20 instead of 15, double water healing, and manually resolve regeneration suppression and ability recovery. Catch and Release falling damage depends on actual distance and collision. |
| Lakshmi | Stotram damage and healing share one activation; flower placement, group size for Path of Light, orb consumption, and Lamia Queen protection require encounter state. Haste, Holy Guard, Revered Weapon and orb effects have recipient buffs. Pulse of Life's healing depends on damage actually dealt, not potential rolled damage. |

Supplemental class references: [Judge Magister](https://finalfantasyd20.com/classes/core-classes/knight/archetypes/judge-magister/) and [Knight](https://finalfantasyd20.com/classes/core-classes/knight/).
