# Group 2: v0.1.49

Ten source slugs, ten statblocks. Phase headings on the primal pages describe encounter transitions, not additional actors. Checked with the scoped group draft and its hydrated actor JSON; no live Foundry session or pack compilation.

| Creature | Table operation or source qualification |
| --- | --- |
| Leviathan | Track Ferocious Current stacks, fifth-stack reset, vulnerability/absorption, forced movement, the ship's position and planar transition. Select the appropriate stack/phase roll and enable burst damage when applicable. |
| Magic Urn | The GM consumes the offered X-Potion, increments the percentile threshold and awards treasure/XP on success. Escape does not relocate a token automatically. The published dash is no attack. Skill Focus names two specializations. |
| Odin | Place and remove the three Gungnirs and five Hall of Stone circles. Resolve next-round attacks and attacker eligibility manually. The source gives Shin-Zantetsuken as 20% / 71 HP or 15 rounds; the stated HP is not 20% of either printed total. Bolstering, mirrored and light fortification are descriptive armor properties, not automated triggers. |
| Orothkamila | Choose one gaze at a time; apply actual Drain Eye healing, poison, Statue and shattering to the affected target. The listed +34 natural armor would give AC 41; the actor honors printed AC 31, touch 7, flat-footed 30. |
| Phoenix Spawn | Shroud-on and shroud-off attacks are separate choices. The detailed Shroud DC 27 takes precedence over the aura header's DC 25. Constant Detect Magic and See Invisibility are in a separate book. Arcana is the selected second Knowledge skill. Wall of fire is named by the feat but not granted by the spell list. Track annual resurrection, permanent negative level, regeneration suppression and healing based on damage actually taken. |
| Phoenix, the Immortal Bird | Place patches and tethers; track cleanses, brand transfer/doubling, summons, Revelation stacks and revival percentages. Brand tick options cover 2 to 64 dice; continue doubling manually beyond 64. Refresh the summon charge once per phase. |
| Ramuh | Track orb count (maximum five), insulation/resistance, arbiters and their HP, invulnerability while charging, High Tide and charm removal. Select Judgment Bolt damage by surviving arbiter count. Phase I Lightning Armor lists both 15 rounds and 10 rounds for allies. |
| Ravana | Position directional telegraphs and the 30-ft. safe center, move tethered orbs, count incoming attacks during defense and choose the appropriate follow-up. Summon quantities are rollable; token placement and phase recharge are manual. |
| Reaper | Check HD divisibility before resolving Lvl. 5 Death. Apply Doom as Countdown, not the Doom spell, and apply Rend Armor damage to the equipment, not its wearer. |
| Shemhazai | Select exactly one bolt element on each burst routine. Grit return fire spends one point for both attacks. Decide parry success, reposition the token and resolve concealment and overlapping Soul Purge explosions individually. |

The independent group comparisons retain the original audit values. Cases the stock audit cannot project (such as an item whose first action charges but whose second action rolls damage) are documented only after checking the corresponding hydrated mode or separate routine.
