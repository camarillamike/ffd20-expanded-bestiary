## Table adjudication

- **Shiva:** switch the active form buff and use its weapon and feats. Track the Dreams of Ice turn counter; every fifth-round die-step increase, actual duration of resistance reduction, and persistent icy terrain need adjudication. Heavenly Strike requires the per-target attack penalty. Avalanche and Glass Dance are resolved per icicle or target.
- **Susano:** enter party size and creatures in range for Brightstorm. Orb movement and tether targets, swapping prisons, wave placement, clash successes, initiative changes, and counters are not scene automation. The named katana carries its enchantment notes; its special properties do not automatically apply to targets.
- **Titan:** choose the phase-dependent number of Tumult pulses, boulders, and rock targets. Place the prison and terrain; the new phase and counters are not automatic.
- **Vorpal Bunny:** record actual damage before the survivor save, transfer drained HP, and apply the rolled ability damage and special death exception manually.
- **Yakshi:** apply the resistance penalties, once-per-target daily protection, drowning checks, and Malediction healing to the correct creatures. The contradictory Perception header is retained in a note; skills use +22.
- **Yojimbo:** manually resolve movement, chain kills, initiative, gil-based gaze penalties and death or reduction to 1 HP. Kozuka has separate phase cooldowns; use only the active phase.
- **Black Cait:** track gil payments and choose the appropriate recipient buff. Telekinetic tracks up to five objects; set remaining objects on activation. Variable weapon details, immunities, target wealth, spell resistance, MP refunds, and the hat's special properties require adjudication. Dark Arts has separate enhanced and ordinary spell actions. Header dark CL 33 is used for variable damage where applicable; the passive text itself only explains +5 of the +7 increase. Spell duration text is not a combat timer.
- **Ancient Castle Blue Dragon:** MP damage is rolled separately from HP. Enter actual damage for reflection. Status swaps, status deflection, drowning progression, and phase-II healing are not automatic.
- **Cocytus:** resolve each Miasma follow-up save separately; assign Levinbolt conditions per hit. Disable the included airborne defense bonus when not defending in flight.
- **Clockwork Dragon:** the explosion has separate fire and slashing damage; destruction on its next turn and winding expiry are GM-triggered.
