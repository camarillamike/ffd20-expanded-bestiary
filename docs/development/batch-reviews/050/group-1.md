**Reference decisions.** Both machines use Skills Perception +21 over Senses +20; “has TRACKS” is not part of Sense Motive +21. “Lighting Reflexes” uses Lightning Reflexes. Presence of Mind is an explicit local interpretation using the [identically named third-party initiative feat](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/feats/3rd-party-feats/4-winds-fantasy-gaming/combat-feats/presence-of-mind-combat/); the encounter gives no publisher. Its Intelligence contribution is included once in the printed +12. Mateus's named talents were reviewed against the [Black Mage talent table](https://finalfantasyd20.com/classes/core-classes/black-mage/mage-talents/). It omits Twincast; the local older-talent ruling uses the same 1/day, combined-MP-at-most-CL rule found on both white mage and necromancer advanced talents. This is not the combat feat or the separately owned Doublecast.

**Runtime boundary.** Crystal-magic and innate casting link to spell rules; resolve their effects without charging ordinary MP. Field items have capacity and instructions, but automatic absorption, critical immunity, fast healing 20 and 24-hour shutdown need the shared runtime allowlist. Elemental absorption, conditional saves and reductions not directly applied by the sheet remain GM decisions; no live-runtime success is claimed.
