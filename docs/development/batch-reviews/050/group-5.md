## MOAB supplements and runtime

- [Spell chart](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1NbKtKkSfRS0gtSmbM0mw2pcTyAFcLG6xJTBi75H1W1c/edit): all 190 leveled entries independently compared by template, level and name, plus five common cantrips. Corrected "Bure II" to Burn II and "Umbra Spikes III" to Umbral Spikes III; parentheses on the Ver spells are preserved as chart context rather than extra classes. The 195 entries are not 195 universally castable spells: 118 have published rule pages, 77 are reference-only with no cast action. Parent owns runtime casting gate and spell authoring.
- [Template chart](https://drive.google.com/open?id=14g9pIC8eC4faVDOHwy38_Dk0JnedkHiukjTnlBMYUwg) and [summon table](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1j7QoTy5__NhAAY-jX6Rg9IL2jJXMXKlf66UWSXghDoQ): a template's DR, flat resistance, absorption and forward half-resistance are exposed on the toggle. Parent runtime must consume those flags; the older bomb helper grants immunity and is not equivalent. Prismatic absorption is separate, recipient-scoped. No bolt DC or rank-I Prismatic Spikes retaliation formula is supplied by the creature/template page. Do not substitute a mount's printed DC. Resolve those at table; also handle cross-actor HP/MP transfers, summons and suppression.
- [Powerful Maneuvers](https://finalfantasyd20.com/system-rules/optional-systems/moogle-in-the-room/) is the FFd20 optional feat: +2 to the four initiating maneuvers, not an unconditional CMB/CMD increase.
