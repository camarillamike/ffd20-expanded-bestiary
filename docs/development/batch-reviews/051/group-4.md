## Apply handoff

- Kaiser: the nine Barrier Shift buffs supply actor-local `affinity051.absorb/weak/half`; empty arrays mean no affinity in that category. Life Force supplies only `half`, and applies to incoming physical/magical attacks. Check precedence with elemental absorption, no double half, and nonstandard/untyped magic. Runic absorbs spells rather than damage types; Phase Link is incorporeality, not universal half. The group runtime gates source-heading actions and elemental breath modes. For the fifth fight, disable elemental buffs and enable Last Form. Select the two inherited burst buffs manually; clear outdated burst buffs and restore HP/MP at transitions.
- Ark phase III: `Plasma Shield: active` gates the separate `Plasma Shield` item (tag `plasma-shield`, 410 capacity). While positive, subtract damage here first, carry excess to Ark, and suppress critical hits. Heal the shield 41/round up to 410, separately from Ark. At zero it shuts down and cannot return for 24 hours. Test overflow, then healing during lockout. The globes are not shields. Lapis Lazuli or the Plasma Shield action enables the field. The group runtime serializes shield hits, applies overflow after mitigation and handles recovery. At lockout expiry capacity returns, but activation is explicit. For a critical hit entered as a bare number without ordinary attack rolls, supply ordinary damage manually.
- Ark's Cruise Chaser GA-100/Photon secondary Will DC 39: failed recipient suffers shadow resistance -50 for 2d4+1 rounds; if originally immune, take at most 50 shadow per ability; if originally absorbing, become immune instead. Recipient reminder provided. This requires the **previous shadow defense** and must not clear absorption or immunity to other elements. Holy Bleed likewise needs recipient-aware handling; ordinary affinity arrays cannot encode either numeric exception.
- Cross-target effects, counters, dispels, arena movement, phase sequencing, unprinted DCs, percentage/current-HP assignments, special gear triggers, and burst-specific Hasteja/Shell/regen effects require GM review in a live scene. A zero-open draft is not an end-to-end Apply test. The group runtime debits paid Kaiser/Omega SLAs after use, activates Omega's one-round exposure, and replaces the native DR row. Renewal advances the life and restores HP/MP. Use it on defeat only; do not Apply its healing card again. Reset its two charges for a new encounter.
