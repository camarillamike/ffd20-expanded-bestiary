## Rulings and live smoke

- Scaling thresholds use current/max HP. Two printed conflicts: Ultima's Retribution says below 50% but prints 840 rather than 700 of 1400; Zero's Gigaflare says 50% but prints 1176 rather than 1470 of 2940. Use the percentage; phase buffs can be set manually. Zodiark enters III at or below 520/1560. Phase changes persist through healing. Reset encounter state is not healing or a recharge reset.
- Shinryu: Driving Gale is required for Maelstrom but undefined on the page. Confirm eligibility at the table. Assign Roulette targets/numbers before rolling. Roll Atomic Ray follow-ups only for distinct targets; apply recipient buffs, healing and conditional Shell saves to the correct actors. The empowered wave gives length but no width; use a line and agree its width. Hasteja's extra strike is selectable from the natural attacks, not an extra action.
- Ultima: check that the pair chooser fires two owned standard-action spells, charges their normal costs, and allows independent targets. Apply the overlap-only -2 save and paired-spell +2 penetration manually. Confirm shield spikes only on a qualifying successful melee hit.
- Zodiark: after an actual weakness hit, roll once, then select the matching barrier. Test shadow/holy replaced by fire/water (ice Strong); overlapping Greater Barrier must halve once only. Confirm burst duration and physical half through the parent damage dispatcher. HD filters, float terrain and the once-per-phase healing bonus from Faith are adjudicated at the table.
- Neo: the GM confirms a qualifying absorbed attack, then uses Energy Orb to fill a slot. Orbs are boss-owned trackers, **not force fields or separately placed tokens**; the page supplies no size, initiative or HD. In phase III resolve the attack vs orb AC, enter each damage type on that orb, and use its Destroyed card to apply recoil at the recorded power. Resolve simultaneous destructions before the immediate absorption of survivors. Orb age and one shot/round are checked in combat; select age manually outside combat. Check no orb HP is deducted from the boss and Flare II cannot cast with 7 MP.
- Zero: choose/track designated opponents and verify the hit that triggers each reaction. Phase II allows two designated opponents but only one counter per round. Phase III hides the glow; do not reveal the element to players when selecting it. Manual target adjustments: Glare treats immunity as half and resistance as 10 lower; Giga/Teraflare bypass DR and Evasion. Disintegrate a creature or object reduced to zero by Teraflare even on a successful save.
