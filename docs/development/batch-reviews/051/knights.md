# Knights of the Round: companion review

The [FFd20 encounter](https://finalfantasyd20.com/gamemastering/bestiary/bosses/primal-family/knights-of-the-round/) includes twelve separate CR 20 knights after King Thordan. Each has its own HD, HP, senses, armor, strikes, equipment, features, portrait and prototype token. The FFd20 statblock names are retained. Artwork provenance, including alternate spellings, is recorded separately.

| Knight | Distinguishing sheet or roll check |
| --- | --- |
| Adelphel | Seven attacks at the same -5 penalty on Holy Bladedance. His Cover interception and shield are separate. |
| Charibert | Immediate Elemental Shield, three uses per day; the recipient's resistance is 20 to six elements, not Holy or Shadow. Heavensflame's initial burst and five-round hazard are separate modes. |
| Grinnaux | Dimensional Collapse has its own save and residual damage. Hyperdimensional Slash explicitly notes its exceptional interaction with Shadow immunity for GM resolution. |
| Guerrique | Four Wind Shear attacks; his +2 Greataxe uses FFd20 [Roaring Burst](https://finalfantasyd20.com/magical-items/magical-weapons/roaring-burst/), including 2d10 Wind on a confirmed x3 critical. |
| Hammeric | Flash Rain rolls both Ice and Water. Flash Rain and Hiemal Storm have separate Ability Focus selections. The Auto-Regen roll is capped at the remaining gap to half HP. |
| Hermenost | Lightning Strike is an alternative on the owned warhammer. Conviction Towers resolves on the following round, with stagger even on a successful save. |
| Ignasse | Armor Training modifies his own armor. Skyward Leap, Earthen Glaive, and Magnitude 8 are separate actions. |
| Janlenoux | Shield Ward contributes to touch AC while the shield is worn; Unwavering Confidence is a toggleable Constitution bonus. |
| Nourdenet | Power Staff uses its compendium range. Laser Barrage varies the hit count per enemy; Holy Meteor distinguishes placement distance from burst radius. |
| Paulecrain | Bloodreaver has an alternate non-elemental mode against undead or shadow-immune creatures. Shadowrend's printed interval ends in `1d2+`; the sheet labels the missing modifier rather than inventing it. |
| Vellguine | Spear of Fury is seven strikes with a Water rider. The [Jetstream Burst](https://finalfantasyd20.com/magical-items/magical-weapons/) longspear deals Water, not Wind. Spiral Thrust has a separate next-round pattern. |
| Zephirin | Sacrament fires four rays. Sacred Cross loses two dice for each full quarter of maximum HP lost. |
