# Elemental bomb art: v0.1.42

The original Fire portraits remain unchanged. The other 16 portraits were made with the built-in image generation tool, with one transparent-background identity-preserving edit per species and element. No images are generated in Foundry. Packaged portraits and prototype tokens use the same local WebP.

## Prompt set

Common direction: Use case: identity-preserve. Make a finished square transparent Foundry VTT token portrait. Image 1 is the edit target and sole species reference. Preserve the specific creature's silhouette, facial proportions, gaze, mouth shape, original attitude and viewing angle. Retain the style of the reference as recognizable game creature artwork. Redesign the palette and crest material only: [element direction below]. Show the entire floating creature with even transparent padding on all four sides, subject takes about 80% of square. Make the distinct crest shape readable at 48px. Remove any background from the reference. No ground, cast shadow on ground, scenery, lettering, labels, border, or token frame. True transparent alpha.

Bombino: Keep the characteristic widely open dark zigzag mouth and squinting striped eyes. Cherry Bomb: Use the original Cherry Bomb as the reference, preserving its small hands, stocky crown, upright expression and small wavy mouth, not Bombino's oversized zigzag grin.

- Ice: glacial pale cyan, frost-white faceted crystal tips and crisp frosted edges.
- Water: rich marine blue and turquoise; a rounded breaking wave crest and watery specular ripple, absolutely no ice or crystals.
- Wind: fresh mint and emerald; swept-back curling gust-shaped crown, light wisplike side fins.
- Earth: warm copper and ochre, layered rock plates with one strong angular ridge and pale stone edge.
- Lightning: electric gold and lemon yellow with a bold forked lightning crest and small cobalt shadow accents.
- Holy: pearl ivory, warm gold outlines and a sunburst crown, regal golden light, no lightning forks.
- Dark: deep indigo and plum; smoky swept shadow tips and saturated magenta edge-light so face reads on dark maps.
- Arcane: opalescent lavender and cyan with one luminous rose-violet geometric arcane diamond on the crown; clear jewel-like glyph, distinct from Dark.

Reviewed at 64px alongside the Fire originals. Export: square 512px WebP with alpha and consistent margins.
