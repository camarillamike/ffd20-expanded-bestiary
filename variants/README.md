# Elemental Bomb forms

As GM, open **Bombino**, **Cherry Bomb**, or **Bomb T1** through **T4** and choose **Create Bomb Form** on the actor sheet. Select the element and choose **Create Actor**. Foundry adds a separate world actor; the original keeps its artwork, spells, and defenses.

The available forms are Fire, Ice, Wind, Earth, Lightning, Water, Holy, Dark, and Arcane. Some higher-tier forms have their own names, such as **Mystical (Arcane)** for Bomb T3. The JSON files here are the templates used by the sheet; you do not need to import them manually.
